How we rate

Not a hunch. The same five steps for every shirt.

Agents do the legwork, people check it, and every claim links to its source. Anyone can see every step on GitHub.

1 Agent Collect Who is on every shirt this season: front, back and sleeve. Every club, every season
2 Agent Trace Who owns each sponsor, all the way up to a state or a fund. Company registries, reports
3 Agent + you Find evidence Human-rights reports tied to that owner. Every claim gets a source. UN, NGOs, courts, press
4 Person Review A person checks each claim and source before anything counts. Nothing goes live unchecked
5 Automatic Publish The rating goes live and the change is logged for everyone to see. Full history, public
What the levels mean
CleanChecked. Nothing found.
SpottedA lesser link to a state.
StainedA serious sponsor on the front.
SoakedA severe sponsor, or two serious ones.
