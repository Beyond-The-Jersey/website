Rating · hover a level
▲ Arsenal

The shirt is Stained: the front sponsor, Emirates, is owned by the Government of Dubai.

Why is that a problem? Dubai is part of the United Arab Emirates, where criticising the government can land you in prison. In 2024, 43 activists got life sentences in a single mass trial. Putting Emirates on a famous shirt makes the country look good to millions of fans while that goes on. That’s called sportswashing. Human Rights Watch, Jul 2024 ↗
